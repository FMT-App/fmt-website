Our Story

Built by a player,
for players.

FMT started with a simple idea: football players deserve to understand their performance, not just the result of a match.

As a football player myself, I noticed how difficult it can be for amateur players to properly track their development. Professional players have coaches, analysts, statistics and detailed performance data. Amateur players often don't have access to these tools.

I wanted to change that.

I started building Football Match Tracker to give amateur players a simple way to record their matches, track their statistics, understand their performance and see their progress over time.

What started as an idea became a project I have been developing step by step, with the goal of creating something that can genuinely help football players take their development more seriously.

FMT is built by a player, for players. This is only the beginning.
